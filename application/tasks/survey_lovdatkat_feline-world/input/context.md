# LovDatKat — Feline World Product Test

## Product concept

LovDatKat is developing a premium scarf collection centered on sophisticated feline-inspired design.

The goal is to create scarves that feel elegant, stylish, collectible, and wearable first, while incorporating cat motifs in a way that can feel subtle, clever, or visually surprising.

Some designs may reveal the feline theme immediately, while others may look like classic luxury prints from a distance and reveal cats only upon closer inspection.

## Concepts being tested

### Le Chat Classique
Refined small-scale feline motifs with a timeless, polished look. Classic navy, cream, black, ivory, and structured borders.

### Purr & Pour
Playful but sophisticated feline motifs inspired by café culture, coffee, cocktails, conversation, and city life.

### Desert Cat
Feline design combined with Arizona western and desert influences such as open landscapes, stars, turquoise-inspired details, warm earth tones, and refined western styling.

### Catwalk
Fashion-forward feline imagery inspired by New York and Paris style, runway culture, polished tailoring, and metropolitan elegance.

### Wild at Heart
A bolder glamorous concept using feline energy, leopard/jaguar-inspired visual language, dramatic pattern, and a more confident Los Angeles nightlife feeling.

## Product direction

The collection should avoid looking childish, novelty-driven, costume-like, or overly literal.

The desired experience is:

- premium
- stylish
- sophisticated
- memorable
- giftable
- expressive
- wearable
- visually distinctive

The brand is especially interested in designs where the customer first notices an attractive pattern and only later discovers the feline details.

## Target customer

Adults who enjoy fashion accessories, expressive personal style, distinctive prints, gifting, collectible fashion, cats, or feline-inspired design.

The test should not assume that all respondents are cat owners.

We want to understand whether the designs can appeal both to dedicated cat lovers and to fashion-oriented consumers who simply appreciate the visual language.

## What this survey is probing

- strongest overall concept
- purchase intent
- wearability
- perceived quality
- perceived premium value
- gift appeal
- visual distinctiveness
- emotional reaction
- cat-lover appeal
- broader fashion appeal
- preferred design direction
- preferred color direction
- price sensitivity
- likelihood of stopping to look while scrolling
- whether subtle feline motifs are more appealing than obvious cat imagery

## Research goal

Identify which Feline World concept has the strongest potential for further design development and real-world market testing.

This survey is exploratory and should help LovDatKat decide which concept or concepts deserve investment in prototypes, imagery, pricing tests, and launch development.
