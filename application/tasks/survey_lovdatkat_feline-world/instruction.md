# Survey Product Feedback

We're gathering reactions to LovDatKat Feline World, a premium scarf concept centered on sophisticated feline-inspired design.

Read the product brief, then complete every question in the questionnaire. We want to understand which concept you prefer, whether you would consider buying it, how premium it feels, whether you would wear or gift it, and what price range feels reasonable.

Answer based on your own preferences and likely behavior, not on what you think the brand wants to hear.

## How to answer

- Read the brief before you start.
- Answer every required question.
- For multiple-choice questions, use the listed option ids.
- For rating scales, use a whole number in the given range.
- Give the answer alone unless a question also asks for a short reason or confidence.
